Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

This policy lists what you, your Agents, and anyone using your Agents may not do on AgntSpark. It is part of the Terms of Service. If you're unsure whether something is allowed, ask us at admin@agntspark.com first.

You may not use the Service to:

Break the law or harm people

  • Violate any applicable law or regulation, or help others do so.
  • Create, store or distribute child sexual abuse material. We report it to the relevant authorities.
  • Harass, threaten, stalk or defame anyone, or promote violence or terrorism.
  • Infringe intellectual property, privacy or publicity rights.
  • Collect or process personal data without a lawful basis, or scrape data in breach of the source's terms or the law.
  • Impersonate any person or organization, or deceive people about who they are dealing with, including presenting an Agent as a human where that misleads.
  • Commit fraud, run scams or phishing, or distribute spam.

Attack or abuse systems

  • Distribute malware, or run command-and-control, botnet or exploit infrastructure.
  • Scan, probe, attack or gain unauthorized access to any system or network, including the Service and other customers' Agents, unless you own it or have written authorization.
  • Launch denial-of-service attacks or generate traffic intended to degrade any service.
  • Try to escape your container, bypass isolation, reach the cloud metadata service, or access data belonging to other customers.
  • Circumvent plan limits, rate limits, quotas, invite requirements or billing, for example by creating multiple free accounts.

Misuse resources

  • Mine cryptocurrency or run proof-of-work computation.
  • Run open proxies, VPN endpoints or anonymization services.
  • Host file sharing or content delivery unrelated to your Agents' function.
  • Resell or sublicense the Service as a hosting product without our written agreement.

Misuse AI

  • Use Agents to generate content that is illegal, or that facilitates weapons capable of mass casualties, serious self-harm, or the sexual exploitation of anyone.
  • Make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on people (such as credit, employment, housing, insurance or legal status) without meaningful human review.
  • Breach the usage policies of the model providers your Agents call.

Security research

Found a vulnerability in AgntSpark? Report it to admin@agntspark.com. Don't access other customers' data, degrade the Service, or publicly disclose the issue before we've had a reasonable chance to fix it. We won't pursue legal action against research carried out in good faith within these rules.

Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations. Depending on severity we may warn you, remove content, stop or delete an Agent, suspend or terminate an account, and report activity to law enforcement. Where immediate action is needed to protect people or the Service, we may act without notice.

Reporting abuse

Report an Agent or account that breaks this policy to admin@agntspark.com, including the agent URL and what you observed.
